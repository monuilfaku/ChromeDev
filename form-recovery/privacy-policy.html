Privacy Policy

Form Recovery: Automatic Form Backup & Recovery Extension

Last Updated: September 2026

Our Absolute Privacy Guarantee

Form Recovery operates 100% locally on your computer. We do not collect, transmit, monetize, or inspect any of your data. No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking cookies, and zero external server connections. What you type stays inside your personal browser sandbox.

1. Overview & Single Purpose

Form Recovery is designed for a single, narrow purpose: to safeguard user-typed web inputs against accidental browser crashes, session timeouts, and closed tabs, providing one-click recovery when needed.

2. Data Storage & Architecture

All backed-up form inputs are stored strictly within Chrome's local storage sandbox (chrome.storage.local).

  • Local Scope: Drafts are scoped deterministically to origin + pathname so session query parameters or random hashes never create duplicated or leaked entries.
  • Zero Remote Transmission: Form inputs are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud infrastructure.
  • Auto-Pruning: Drafts older than the user-configured retention period (1, 3, 7, or 30 days) are pruned automatically by a local background service worker.

3. Strict Privacy Exclusions (What We NEVER Save)

Form Recovery employs a strict multi-layer filter that deliberately refuses to save any sensitive credentials:

  • Passwords: Any element with type="password" or type="hidden" is completely ignored.
  • Payment & Card Data: Fields with autocomplete tokens such as cc-number, cc-csc, cc-exp, or payment tokens are excluded.
  • Authentication & Security Tokens: One-time passcodes (one-time-code / OTP / 2FA), PIN numbers, and SSN fields are never recorded.
  • Explicitly Excluded Fields: Elements marked with data-private, data-sensitive, or inside form[autocomplete="off"] with sensitive keyword patterns.
  • Domain Blacklist: Users can exclude entire domains (and their subdomains) from ever being tracked.

4. Chrome Permissions Disclosures

Form Recovery requests only the minimal permissions required to provide its core service:

  • storage: Required to persist form drafts and user settings locally within chrome.storage.local.
  • alarms: Required to run a daily background maintenance schedule that automatically cleans up drafts older than your configured retention policy.
  • Host Permission (<all_urls>): Required because users fill out forms across diverse web domains. Without broad host access, the extension cannot know where user forms need crash protection.

5. User Data Control & Deletion

You maintain complete ownership and control over your saved form drafts:

  • Selective Deletion: Delete drafts for individual sites directly from the extension popup.
  • Full Data Wipe: The Options page features a "Delete All Data" action that instantly erases all stored drafts from your browser.
  • Uninstall Cleanup: Removing the extension automatically and permanently deletes all data stored by Form Recovery.

6. Changes & Contact

Any updates to this privacy policy will be reflected directly on this page. If you have any inquiries regarding this policy or the extension's privacy architecture, please submit an issue on the official project repository.
